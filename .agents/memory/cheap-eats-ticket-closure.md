---
name: Cheap Eats ticket closure
description: User-selected retention behavior for the private ticket close flow.
---

After the transcript copy is saved, show a 5-second countdown, then delete the Discord ticket channel and its original messages. Keep the archived transcript.

**Why:** The user selected channel deletion after being told the transcript remains while the source channel and its messages are removed.

**How to apply:** Do not delete a ticket channel until transcript delivery succeeds. If Discord refuses deletion, restore the active ticket state and report the required permission.
